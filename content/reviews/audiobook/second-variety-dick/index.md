PKD is a master of a certain kind of paranoid vision.  He was obsessed with the aftermath of World War 3, self-replicating weapons, the reality of a man.  All these themes come together in what I believe is his best short story, a taut thriller of soldiers surviving in an atomic wasteland inhabited by autonomous robot Claws. I won't spoil the ending, but it is short, most excellent, and available for free at [www.manybooks.net](http://www.manybooks.net/titles/dickp3203232032.html). Why haven't you read it already?
