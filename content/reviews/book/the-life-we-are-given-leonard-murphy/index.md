Leonard and Murphy are two of the founders of the human potential movement, using the retreat center at Esalen which they also co-founded as a launching pad into a lifelong exploration of what it means to grow across many dimensions. The pair draw primary inspiration from sports medicine, Eastern religious practices, and aikido, but unlike a lot of people who followed them in what evolved into the New Age movement, they remain firmly grounded in consensus reality. While they can't identify specific causal mechanism, much of what do has statistical correlations (backed up in the appendices) and doesn't require anything beyond a link between belief and interior growth.

*The Life We Are Given* is a guidebook to Integral Transformative Practice, a two year experiment that combined much of their life's work. The ITP program begins with a set of four affirmations, three personalized and the fourth "My entire being is balanced, vital, and healthy". The hard work is a set of ITP katas, roughly 45 minutes of stretches, basic yoga, and meditation and visualization which must be done at least five days a week, as well as 180 minutes of moderate cardio weekly, and some recommended strength training.

If you follow the program, Leonard and Murphy promise improvements both normal and metanormal. A longtime runner managed to trim a stubborn two inches off his waistline. An amateur golfer increased her drive distance by 60%. In the metanormal, a businesswoman found her entire line of work become much more fulfilling and profitable without dramatic changes in her practice, and another subject managed to grow almost 2" in height as an adult before pain and weakness caused a stop (health must come first). Doing ITP with a group is very very helpful, but the practice can be attempted solo.

There's nothing in the ITP katas that is insane or particularly exotic.  But the fact is most Americans do not find the time, a little more than an hour a day, to do the work which would keep them healthy and give them everything they want. Hell, I've got nothing but free time right now and I can't find the time.  What is it in us that resists health?
